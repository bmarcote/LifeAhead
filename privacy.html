Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

LifeAhead is designed with privacy at its core. Your data never leaves your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Who We Are

LifeAhead is an iOS application developed and maintained by an indie developer, Soft Synchrotron. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at soft.synchrotron@icloud.com.

2. Information You Provide

LifeAhead may ask you for the following information to personalise your experience:

  • Age — used to calculate your life expectancy chart
  • Gender — used to select the appropriate life expectancy data
  • Country — used to look up country-specific life expectancy statistics
  • Notification preferences — time and frequency of daily messages

All of this information is stored exclusively on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). It is never transmitted to any server, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or analytics purposes.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact information
  • Location data (GPS or IP-based)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to our servers
  • Browsing history or behavioural data
  • Any data from third-party services

4. Notifications

LifeAhead may request permission to send you push notifications. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device — no external server is involved in their delivery. If you deny or later revoke notification permission, the app continues to function normally. You can manage notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

5. Purchases

LifeAhead offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Lifetime Access") processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not handle payment information directly. Apple's own privacy policy governs the purchase transaction. We only receive a confirmation from Apple that the purchase was completed, which is stored locally on your device.

6. Data Storage and Security

All data entered into LifeAhead is stored on your local device using Apple's standard iOS mechanisms. This data is protected by your device's built-in security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We have no access to this data and cannot retrieve or reset it remotely.

If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

LifeAhead is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Third-Party Services

LifeAhead does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or social media plugins. The only external service used is Apple's App Store for processing the optional in-app purchase.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any of your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to retrieve, export, or delete on your behalf. If you wish to remove all data associated with LifeAhead, simply delete the app from your device — this permanently removes all locally stored information.

Questions about this policy?

We are happy to help. Reach out at soft.synchrotron@icloud.com and we will respond as soon as possible.
